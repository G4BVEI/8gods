the attack system works in a kind of simple pattern
we have some elements
    normal = you can defend with anything
    darkness = you can defend with anything(but it instakills you if you take it)
    water = you can only defend with fire
    fire = you can only defend with water
    rock = you can only defend with wood
    wood = you can only defend with rock
    light = you cant defend(you can sometimes but you need to be lucky with your cards)

besides that we have some standart rules
    normal attacks dont stack
    +attacks can be played alone or in top of other attack cards
    if diferent elements get stacked the stack will neutralize or turn into the lesser
        light + (any element besides darkness) = element
        element(NonLight) + element(nonLight) = normal
        light + dark = normal
    after an attack the attacked player gets a "defense turn" in which he can swing back, reflect, or defend the attack
    swing back and reflect cards work on a solitary stance
    defense cards can always stack(besides if he has debuffs)
    certain defense cards apply effects to the attacker(or all oponents) if the person's been hit
    some attacks are dependant on probability and can have multiple side effects
