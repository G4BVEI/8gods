Base
    There can only be up to 8 players in a room
    The objective is to be the last remaining player
    All cards have rarity and price (both integers)
    Theres 4 card types including attack, miracles, defense, consumables and passive cards(revive on death for example)
    There are 2 decks
        Attack deck: includes attacks
        Secondary deck: which include everything else
    The game operates in turns and rounds
        Whenever a player plays we call a turn
        When all players have played(unless skipped, then we jump them) we consider a round
    Any cards(except miracles) can have extra behaviours and be more than one type of card at once
    Attacks are element based
        normal = you can defend with anything
        darkness = you can defend with anything(but it instakills you if you dont defend 100% of it)
        water = you can only defend with fire
        fire = you can only defend with water
        ground = you can only defend with plant
        plant = you can only defend with ground
        light = you can only defend with light
    Defense cards can have element also as stated previously
    Miracle cards consume mana and range behaviour
    Miracle cards remain on the users hand, and can be seem by other players(after they're played)
    Consumables can range from all sorts of behaviour(but generally they heal)
    Some cards can alter the flux of the game temporarily or permanently as a player being skipped or all players being reshuffled
    Some cards have delayed effects
        Ex: A player can be skipped 2 rounds from now
    Some cards have permanent effects in the game, and can vary on triggers
    Whenever a player turn exceeds 35 seconds the player gets kicked
    Anytime a player disconnects a bot enter in his places temporarily
Gameloop
    At the start players are assigned a random order of play, they start with 40 life, 10 mana and 15 money and receive 4 attack cards and 4 secondary cards
    The game follows the play order
    At the start of each turn a card of the following types must be used: attack, miracle or consumable, optionally the player can discard a card of his own to buy a new one
    Any card can be used on anyone (attack self, heal enemy)
    At the end of a turn the player should pick his cards from which deck he chooses (a popup is given)
Attack
    Simple attack cards dont stack
    Cards with an + at the start can be played alone or in top of other attack cards
    Some attacks can be AoE and those never stack, AoE cards tend to hit on probability(card can miss everyone and hit 1 person only and so on)
    If different elements get stacked the stack will neutralize or turn into the lesser
        light + (any element besides darkness) = element
        element x(non-light) + element y(non-light) = normal
        light + dark = normal
        element x + element x = element x
    After an attack the attacked player gets a "defense turn" in which he can swing back, reflect, or defend the attack
    Swing backs and reflect cards cant be stacked
    Swings make the attack go to a random player
    Reflect goes back to the attacker
    Defense cards can always stack(besides if the attacked has debuffs)
    Certain defense cards can apply effects that vary target if the attacked has been hit(ex: 50% damage back, or transform the damage in mana)
    Some attacks are dependant on probability and can have multiple probable effects
    Whenever a player has been damaged by an non AoE-attack he can buy an store item instantly
Store
    Theres a store that at all times(unless affected by a card) will have 2 cards at display and the card can be bought be the card's normal price(unless affected by a card)
Curses
    Diseases
        Diseases get worse naturally, with 5% chance per turn to get worse.
            Cold
                1 damage on end of turn (Fever if worsened)
            Fever
                2 damage on end of turn (Hell if worsened)
            Hell
                5 damage on end of turn (Heaven if worsened)
            Heaven
                HP+5 on end of turn (HP0 if worsened)
